I am currently a Postdoc at the [AI4I CRYOTO Lab](https://ai4i.it/rde-labs/#CRYPTO) in Turin under the supervision of [Tamer Mour](https://tamermorr.github.io/). My research areas are lattice-based cryptography and provable security both in the classical and in the quantum setting.
I obtained a PhD at [DTU](https://www.compute.dtu.dk/Sections/CybSec) under the supervision of [Christian Majenz](https://www.christianmajenz.info/about-me.html). I completed my Master’s degree at Pisa University under the supervision of [Emmanuela Orsini](https://cseao.github.io/) working on isogeny-based cryptography.
